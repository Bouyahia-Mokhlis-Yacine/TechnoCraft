# SATIM Multi-Resolution Sequential Stream Detection

**Decision · 27 November 2026.** Enable the method for supervised historical replay and early-warning research. The archive supports a useful distinction between some persistent intraday disruptions and daily closure. Short, sparse-channel interruptions remain difficult to distinguish from ordinary zero arrivals. The Hierarchical Probabilistic Detection with Directional CUSUM model stays authoritative for official merchant-day predictions. Likelihood-Based Collective Anomaly Detection and the Probabilistic–Isolation Forest Hybrid retain their advisory roles.

## Chronology and provenance

- **Reference fit, 1–21 September 2026.** The reference uses eligible ordinary training merchant-days and their transactions. Each replay begins after its reference cutoff.
- **Warning calibration, 22 September–2 October.** Day maxima of eligible scores by detector set thresholds before the held-out overlays.
- **Ordinary evaluation, 3–16 October.** The 14 historical days cover 490 merchant-days. Warning burden on this period is an operational proxy. This archive has no independent feed or incident log.
- **Labelled development, 17–24 October.** The 280 merchant-days include 31 positive days. These labels establish daily overlap only. They supply neither intraday incident onset nor recovery times.

Only the supplied training archive entered the SATIM study. The official test files were read solely by the pre-existing sealed competition parity verification. The original augmentation study's 100%, 31.3% and 2.1% figures are paired incremental detection rates on 48 synthetic CHAPS-style 15-minute interruption fixtures at five-minute, hourly and daily resolution. Those simulations used resolution-specific ordinary day-maximum cutoffs and a known generated settlement profile. A hit required an alert at any eligible close after onset on the injection day, with no post-onset alert on the paired ordinary tape. The daily detection delay was based on one hit. The rates cannot be transferred to SATIM's sparse merchant or channel streams.

Daily expectation \(D\) shrinks the observed weekday rate toward the cell's overall prior rate. The profile share is \(s_b=(n_b+k p_b)/(\sum_b n_b+k)\). Global, channel and category–channel profiles supply the successively pooled priors. Each share sums to one, and scope expectations are coherent sums of cell expectations. ## Detection and incident lifecycle

- **Instantaneous counts.** Inclusive Poisson tails yield \(-\log(p)\) scores for drops and surges. The research compares fixed five-minute and cumulative day-to-time windows. The live candidate tests non-overlapping adaptive windows after three expected events. Channels check at 15-minute boundaries. Unsupported windows expire after six hours.
- **Sequential counts.** For multiplier \(r\in\{0.5,1.75\}\), take one log-likelihood increment \(N_t\log r-(r-1)\mu_t\) per finalized five-minute bin. Accumulate upward and downward scores separately with a floor of zero. Reset intraday state at the archive day boundary or an incomplete bin. The separately maintained frozen daily state advances once at each complete day closure.
- **Interarrival.** When arrivals stop, integrate piecewise-constant intensity over event time. The Poisson no-arrival probability is \(\exp(-\Lambda)\). This measures surprise relative to ordinary exposure. The timer keeps checking through silent intervals.
- **Outcomes and amounts.** Beta–Binomial tails use a shrunk rejection or cancellation rate and a floor of 12 attempts per non-overlapping 30-minute bin. The replay presents accepted flow, median tickets and empirical two-sided amount rarity as descriptive evidence. Negative amounts retain count and quality semantics and carry no value. Contactless magnitudes are preserved and the 2,500 DZD rule is a separate transaction observation.
- **Joint warnings.** The active candidate takes the maximum of the gated count tails, directional CUSUM, integrated gap score and outcome tails by symptom. One cutoff is calibrated on ordinary daily maxima across the complete scope, symptom and time search. Separate cutoffs train each comparator on the same day-level design. This handles the dependence of overlapping tests empirically. There is no theoretical fleet guarantee.
- **Collective.** Fifteen-minute scans use actual merchant exposures. The directional Poisson likelihood searches affected merchant subsets for declines in activity. A Binomial likelihood with a prespecified odds grid scans rejection excess. The scan uses at least two merchants, minimum local exposure and a search-wide calibration across dates, channels, categories and symptoms. A replay advisory gives its interval, observed and expected magnitude, subset, corroboration and investigation action.

The starting operator budget is one notification per day. Calibration compares nominal budgets of 0.5, 1 and 2 by taking empirical quantiles of fleet-wide day maxima and using strict exceedance. The proxy budgets allocate exceedance days between individual and collective searches. The number of openings must still be checked by replay. A warning opens immediately at a crossing, becomes persistent after additional high evidence and resolves after three quiet observations below 65% of the opening threshold. The warning ID groups updates by scope and symptom. Missing completeness marks an existing warning uncertain and suppresses new statistical evidence.

## Held-out incidents

The case ledger runs paired overlays on ordinary October background dates. POS reductions combine six durations from five minutes through six hours with five reductions from 10% through 100%. The study also includes outcomes, surges, monetary overlays, schedule and cold-start controls, compound, gradual and intermittent changes, collective changes and same-day backlog. The profile-driven peak fixtures are separate from random onset fixtures. The ledger records actual changed events, prior expected exposure, simulated window, paired threshold crossings and count change at daily close.

A paired hit requires a threshold exceedance in the affected scope and symptom during the outage or up to one hour after it, with the ordinary control below the same threshold on that tick. The frozen daily engine is compared at closure on newly flagged affected merchant-days. This distinguishes early recall from daily catch. A no-change overlay stays in the denominator and is visible in the case ledger. Delay starts at injection onset in simulated event time and is conditional on a hit. The score-clearance field marks the first later quiet score within the short observation horizon. It is distinct from lifecycle recovery. Event precision cannot be estimated from every interval of the archive because genuine operational onset and recovery are unknown. ## Limits and next research gate

The hierarchy and daily thresholds serve different purposes. The active intraday configuration is a research policy with a small ordinary calibration sample. Five-minute and 60-minute collective scans remain research challengers. Sustained monetary shifts, intraday outcome sequential banks and collective or hybrid enrichment at simulated daily closure remain unimplemented. The archived serving path still provides the existing daily advisories. A future authorized feed needs a late-event and correction contract plus independent operational telemetry to establish causes. Fixed-interval count flags and cumulative methods without independent corroboration should remain challenger evidence. A promotion decision needs fresh ordinary dates, measurable workload targets, independently timestamped outcomes and validation of the improved merchant-pooled profile.
